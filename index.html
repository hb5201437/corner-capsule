Corner Capsule

A blind box for your walk. Draw a secret place nearby, follow nothing but an arrow and a distance, and find out where you are when you get there.

How it works

  • Pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes and tap Draw a destination.
  • You get one arrow and one number. No map, no street names.
  • When you arrive, the place is revealed. Keep it in your journal with a photo.
  • Leave an anonymous note for the next person a capsule brings there.
