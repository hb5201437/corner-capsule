Privacy Policy

Corner Capsule · Last updated 8 November 2026

HomeSupport中文

Corner Capsule is made by an independent developer. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data. This page explains exactly what the app uses and where it goes.

What stays on your device

  • Your location and route. The app uses your location to pick a nearby destination and point the arrow. Your position and the route you walk are kept only on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it). They are never uploaded.
  • Your journal. Places you have found, your photos and your private notes are stored only on your device.
  • Saved capsules and settings. Stored only on your device.

What is shared through Apple iCloud

Shared features use Apple's CloudKit public database. Records are stored by Apple and are linked only to an anonymous, app-specific identifier that Apple generates. We never see your name, Apple ID or email.

  • Visits. When you arrive at a named place, the app records an anonymous visit: the place's coordinates (rounded to about 10 metres) and the time. This lets the app show how many people a capsule has brought to that place. It does not include where you started or your route.
  • Public notes. If you choose to leave a note for the next visitor, its text is stored with that visit and shown anonymously to other people who arrive at the same place.
  • Friend codes. If you invite a friend, the shared destination is stored under a short code so your friend can join. Codes expire after 48 hours.
  • Reports. If you report a note, the report (the note and the anonymous author identifier) is stored so it can be reviewed. A note reported by two different people is hidden automatically.
  • Notifications. When you save a place to your journal, the app asks iCloud to notify you when someone leaves a new note there. Deleting all journal entries for that place cancels this.

Usage analytics

We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. It receives counts of actions such as "drew a capsule", "arrived" or "saved to journal", along with details like the walk length, travel mode and the type of place (for example "park"). It never receives your location, place names, notes or photos. The user identifier is hashed on your device, so we cannot tell who you are.

Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, your iPhone sends the current capsule directly to your watch, and the watch sends back when you start or arrive. The watch uses its own location and compass to point the arrow; this stays on your devices.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): to choose destinations and point the way.
  • Camera / Photos: only when you choose to add a photo to your journal.
  • Notifications: to tell you when someone leaves a note at a place you visited.

Deleting your data

Delete journal entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To remove a public note you left, email us with the place and the text and we will delete it.

Children

Corner Capsule is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

隐私政策（中文）

转角盲盒 · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 8 日

转角盲盒由独立开发者制作。没有账号、没有广告、不做追踪，也不会出售或分享你的数据。

只留在你设备上的

  • 你的位置和路线：用来抽取附近的目的地、给你指方向。你的位置和走过的路线只保存在你的 iPhone（以及你使用的 Apple Watch）上，不会上传。
  • 足迹：去过的地方、照片和私人备注只保存在你的设备上。
  • 保留的盲盒和设置：只保存在你的设备上。

通过 Apple iCloud 共享的

共享功能使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 公共数据库，数据由 Apple 保存，只和 Apple 为本 App 生成的匿名标识关联。我们看不到你的名字、Apple ID 或邮箱。

  • 到访：到达一个有名字的地点时，App 会记录一次匿名到访：地点坐标（精确到约 10 米）和时间，用来显示有多少人被盲盒带到过这里。不包含你的出发地和路线。
  • 公开留言：如果你选择给下一个人留一句话，这句话会和这次到访一起保存，并匿名显示给之后到达同一地点的人。
  • 好友口令：邀请朋友同盒时，目的地会以一个短口令保存，48 小时后失效。
  • 举报：举报一条留言时，会保存这条留言和它的匿名作者标识，供人工处理。被两个不同的人举报过的留言会自动隐藏。
  • 通知：把一个地点存进足迹时，App 会请 iCloud 在有人在那里留言时通知你。删除这个地点的所有足迹即取消。

使用统计

我们使用注重隐私的统计服务 TelemetryDeck 了解 App 的使用情况，以便改进。它只收到「抽了盒」「到达」「存进足迹」这类操作的次数，以及时长、步行或骑行、地点种类（如「公园」）。不会收到你的位置、地名、留言或照片。用户标识在你的设备上做了哈希处理，我们无法知道你是谁。

Apple Watch

使用手表 App 时，iPhone 会把当前的盲盒直接发到你的手表，手表在你出发或到达时告诉 iPhone。手表用自己的定位和指南针指方向，这些都只在你的设备之间。

删除数据

在 App 里删除足迹，或删除 App，即可清除设备上的全部数据。想删除你留下的公开留言，请发邮件告诉我们地点和内容，我们会删除。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的信息。
