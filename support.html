Common questions

The arrow points the wrong way

Hold the phone flat and away from magnets or metal for a moment so the compass can settle. On devices without a compass, the screen shows a north marker: hold the top of the phone toward north and the arrow shows the way.

I'm there but the capsule didn't open

GPS can drift by tens of metres between buildings. Within 150 m a "I think I'm here" button appears; tap it to open the capsule.

Can I skip a destination?

Tap Stop while walking to pick a new destination or end the walk. Tap Hold to keep it for another day.

How does "walk together" work?

Tap Invite while walking to get a six-character code. Your friend enters it with the people button on the home screen and gets the same secret destination. Codes last 48 hours.

Someone left an offensive note

Tap … next to the note to report it or hide everything from that person. Notes reported by two people are hidden automatically, and I review every report. You can also email me.

Where is my data?

Your journal, photos and route stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for what is shared.

帮助（中文）

有问题、遇到 bug，或者看到不该出现的留言，请发邮件到 hbao5201437@gmail.com。

箭头指错了方向

把手机平放一会儿，远离磁铁和金属，让指南针校准。没有指南针的设备会显示「北」的标记：手机顶部朝北时，箭头就是方向。

到了但盒子没打开

在楼宇之间 GPS 可能偏几十米。距离 150 米以内会出现「我好像到了，开盒」按钮，点一下就能开盒。

可以不去这个地方吗？

散步时点「不走了」可以换一个目的地或结束散步；点「保留」可以改天再去。

怎么和朋友同盒？

散步时点「邀请」得到一个六位口令，朋友在首页点「朋友」按钮输入，就会抽到同一个保密目的地。口令 48 小时内有效。

看到了不友善的留言

点留言旁边的「…」可以举报，或不再看这个人的留言。被两个人举报的留言会自动隐藏，每条举报我都会处理。也可以直接发邮件。
